# Artwork provenance

The approved hero is the user's supplied portrait. The ten chapter scenes, atlas and transparent emblem were generated earlier in this conversation with that portrait as the character and mood reference. They were restored from their saved original PNGs, without regeneration.

The 3:1 banner and two portraits are mechanical crops of the approved hero. WebP variants and icon PNGs are resized/optimized derivatives. All original artwork is retained in artwork/originals/.

No original reference-site logo, map, soundtrack, chapter image, video, contract or runtime is included. This project is independently created, with no claimed affiliation with Pepina or The One Meme RING.
