## 01. The Still Pond

Blue saw possibility. Red noticed the edge. Born together, they have been looking at the same pond very differently ever since.

They came into the world in the same hour, under the same rain. Blue looked across the water and imagined everything that might rise. Red looked at the very same water and considered what might sink. Neither was interested in explaining herself. The pond learned to make room for both.

## 02. The Blue Court

Blue buys when the pond goes quiet. Her bulls hear a starting bell. By midnight, every little blue Pepe has found a banner.

The Blue Court meets beneath rain-streaked windows. The bull commander brings a plan. The little Pepes bring several louder plans. Blue listens to none of them. She has already made her decision, and the court mistakes her silence for a speech.

## 03. The Red Court

Red knows what follows the cheering. Her bears are already waiting. The little red Pepes have packed for a very short celebration.

Across the water, the Red Court keeps its voices low. A bear commander studies the exits. Small red Pepes fold the banners Blue has not yet raised. Red sits behind the velvet curtain with the expression of someone who has seen this ending before.

## 04. The First Candle

Blue called it a beginning. Red called it a warning. Two couriers left the table in opposite directions.

For a moment, there was only a candle between them. Blue moved a piece forward. Red moved hers back. Somewhere outside, a little Pepe decided that this was probably a declaration of war. By the time anyone thought to ask, the drums had started.

## 05. The Candle War

Bulls met bears at Candle Gate. The little Pepes brought every opinion they owned. Above it all, the sisters remained remarkably unimpressed.

Blue banners filled one end of the bridge. Red banners filled the other. The bull commander called it momentum; the bear commander called it overdue. Every little Pepe had an explanation. Neither sister had asked for one.

## 06. The Bull Trap

The gates opened. The bulls charged. Only after the cheering stopped did anyone ask why Red had left the courtyard empty.

It looked like a clear path. That was the trouble with it. The blue commander marched his Pepes through the gate and found a courtyard with no enemy, no victory, and an unpleasantly heavy door. Red watched from the gallery. Her cup had not yet gone cold.

## 07. The Bear Trap

The bears followed the retreat all the way down. Blue was waiting on the other bank. She had apparently kept the receipt.

The Red Court mistook a retreat for a farewell. Below the stair, blue banners rose from the mist and a very patient bull commander stepped into view. Blue rested a hand on the stone rail. It was the closest she came to saying she had told anyone so.

## 08. Across the Water

Then the rain arrived. For once, neither side had a speech prepared. Two very damp couriers crossed the water.

The banners grew heavy. The drums went quiet. One blue Pepe and one red Pepe met at the middle of the crossing with a white cloth between them. The sisters waited on opposite banks, each entirely certain that the other was about to apologize.

## 09. After the Banners

Both sides said they were only drying it. The generals compared their accounts. The sisters disagreed about who had started it.

In the ruined gallery, the little Pepes wrung rain out of their banners. The bull and the bear stood beneath the same roof for the first time all night. Nobody called it peace. That would have required someone to admit there had been a problem.

## 10. Tea Before Dawn

They share the tea. They do not share a view. Outside, two little Pepes are still arguing about the last candle.

By dawn, both sisters had returned to the same table. Blue saw a cup waiting to be filled. Red saw a cup about to be empty. Their kingdoms slept below the window. The argument, naturally, was only taking a short break.
