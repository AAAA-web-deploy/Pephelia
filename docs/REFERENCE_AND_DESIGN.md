# Reference study and adaptation

Reference: https://theonememe.com/ — visually reviewed on 25 October 2026 before this package was assembled.

The reference opens with a clickable map, links ten locations to cinematic chapters, uses a consistent short title/line/paragraph rhythm, and ends with token controls. Large images, serif typography, misted transitions and quiet controls carry the progression. Its market-cap markers supply a sense of advancement.

Pephelia retains the atlas, ten linked chapters, image-led reading, chapter navigation and closing token section. Its map instead tracks fictional story locations. It does not invent market milestones, airdrops, staking or financial promises. The original site's chain, addresses, images, runtime and music are not bundled.

## Character and story
